About Meridian

How stories are ranked

A story climbs when many outlets cover it (the more independent newsrooms agree it matters, the higher it goes), when it is fresh, and when it concerns France or Europe. Then the app mixes topics and regions so you do not get ten stories about the same thing. Stories matching this week's vocabulary theme get a boost.
